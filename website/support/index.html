Welcome to Aksharr

Every child learns differently.

Aksharr brings together the three foundations of literacy — reading, writing, and phonics — in one focused, distraction-free learning experience designed to help children build skills, confidence, and independence.

Whether a child is just beginning to read and write, needs additional practice, or learns differently because of dyslexia, Aksharr aims to give every child the right tools to learn, without making them feel different.

How to Use Aksharr

Aksharr provides three core areas of literacy practice.

📖 Reading

Build reading confidence step by step.

  • Work through three progressive reading levels that gradually move from highly accessible layouts to standard reading formats.
  • Use phonetic word breakdowns to help decode unfamiliar words.
  • Use read-aloud support as an additional learning pathway.
  • Complete reading checkpoints that assess spoken reading and track progress.

The goal is to help children grow beyond a simplified reading environment and develop greater reading confidence.

✏️ Writing

Build the right habits from the start.

  • Practise letters, numbers, and words through tracing activities.
  • Receive real-time tracing accuracy feedback.
  • Follow guided practice to reinforce correct formation.
  • Develop the practice needed to build writing skills and muscle memory.

🔤 Phonics

Turn sounds into a foundation for literacy.

Aksharr includes five interactive phonics games targeting different areas of phonological development.

These activities help children:

  • Connect sounds with letters
  • Practise phonological skills
  • Decode unfamiliar words
  • Develop stronger spelling skills

Designed With Dyslexia in Mind

Dyslexic children aren't less capable of learning. They may simply need the right tools and the right implementation.

Aksharr uses:

  • Accessible layouts
  • Structured progression
  • Phonetic support
  • Auditory reinforcement
  • Interactive practice
  • Real-time feedback

These features are designed to reduce unnecessary barriers while helping children build literacy skills.

While dyslexic learners are at the heart of Aksharr's design, the app is made for every child.

Who Is Aksharr For?

Aksharr is designed for children learning to read, write, and understand sounds.

It can be especially useful for:

  • Early learners
  • Developing readers
  • Children practising handwriting
  • Children building phonics skills
  • Children with dyslexia
  • Parents looking for one complete literacy app

Common Troubleshooting

An activity isn't working as expected

Please close and reopen Aksharr and try the activity again.

If the problem continues, please contact us and tell us which activity you were using and what happened.

Audio or read-aloud support isn't working

Please check that your device's volume is turned on and try the activity again.

If the issue continues, contact us with details about what happened.

My progress doesn't appear as expected

Please close and reopen Aksharr and check your progress again.

If the issue continues, please contact us and describe what you were doing when the problem occurred.
