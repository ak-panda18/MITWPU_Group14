About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Aksharr handles information when you use the Aksharr app.

Aksharr is a literacy learning app designed to help children develop reading, writing, and phonics skills. The app is designed with children in mind, including children who learn differently because of dyslexia.

We aim to handle information responsibly and to use it only for providing, supporting, and improving the Aksharr learning experience.

Information We Collect

Depending on how Aksharr is used, the app may process the following information:

  • Account information such as an email address used for authentication.
  • Child profile information such as name, age, and gender.
  • Learning and progress information, including reading, writing, and phonics performance.
  • Reading session information such as session duration, timestamps, progress, and checkpoint results.
  • Writing information such as tracing progress, accuracy, mistakes, and unlocked learning items.
  • Phonics information such as exercise type, correct answers, attempts, and session information.
  • A parental PIN when one is configured for access to protected analytics information.
  • Profile photographs when a user chooses to add one.
  • Images and documents selected or captured for the Scan & Read feature.
  • Speech information during reading checkpoints, where the child reads aloud for speech recognition.
Aksharr does not use personal information for advertising.

No advertising SDK or advertising identifier was found in the current app project.

How We Use Information

Information processed by Aksharr is used to provide the app's learning and account functionality.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Maintain child profiles.
  • Save reading, writing, and phonics progress.
  • Resume learning activities.
  • Calculate and display learning progress and performance information.
  • Support reading checkpoint evaluation.
  • Personalize aspects of learning progression, including writing content unlocked through practice.
  • Provide reminders and learning-related notifications when enabled.
  • Synchronize supported information across devices through the services described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

Aksharr stores certain information locally on the device so that learning activities and progress can continue to work.

Depending on the feature, locally stored information can include:

  • Child profile information
  • Reading progress and session information
  • Writing progress and tracing information
  • Phonics progress and session information
  • Checkpoint results
  • Recognized words from reading checkpoints
  • Parental PIN information
  • Profile photographs
  • Scanned document images
  • OCR-extracted text
  • PencilKit handwriting data
  • Reminder preferences
  • Streak and app-progress information

Scanned document images, OCR-extracted text, profile photographs, and PencilKit handwriting data are stored locally and were not observed being uploaded to Aksharr's remote services.

Cloud Services and Data Synchronization

Aksharr uses Google Firebase services for account authentication and synchronization of supported learning and profile information.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to manage user accounts and authentication. Depending on the sign-in method used, this can include an email address and authentication information.

Cloud Firestore

Aksharr uses Cloud Firestore to synchronize supported profile and learning information.

Information sent to Firestore can include child profile information, learning session data, reading progress, writing progress, phonics results, checkpoint information, streak information, reminder preferences, and parental-control information.

Firebase and Google services use encrypted network connections for data transmission.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Aksharr uses the microphone during reading checkpoints where a child reads text aloud.

Apple's Speech framework is used to convert the child's speech into text for reading checkpoint evaluation.

The app processes microphone audio during the checkpoint and does not save the raw audio as a persistent audio file.

The current implementation uses Apple's speech recognition service. Live audio may therefore be transmitted to Apple's speech recognition service during these activities.

Recognized words used by the app for checkpoint history are stored as text in the app's local data.

Camera, Photos, and OCR

Aksharr provides a Scan & Read feature that can use the device camera or selected photos to scan documents.

Apple's Vision framework performs the OCR processing on the device.

Scanned images and OCR-extracted text are stored locally in the app's Documents directory and were not observed being uploaded to Aksharr's remote services.

Users can delete scanned documents from within the Scan & Read section of the app.

A profile photograph may also be selected from the photo library or captured using the camera. Profile photographs are stored locally and were not observed being uploaded to Firestore.

Analytics

Aksharr includes its own in-app learning analytics. These analytics are used to display learning progress and performance information within the app.

The current project does not use a third-party analytics service such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, PostHog, or Sentry.

Learning analytics can include information such as reading, writing, and phonics performance, accuracy, session duration, and progress.

Analytics information is displayed within the app's protected analytics area.

Advertising and Tracking

Aksharr does not contain advertising.

No advertising SDKs, IDFA usage, or App Tracking Transparency implementation were found in the current project.

Aksharr does not use children's information for targeted advertising.

Children's Privacy

Aksharr is designed for children and may process children's profile and learning information as part of providing the app's functionality.

This can include information such as a child's name, age, gender, learning progress, reading performance, writing performance, phonics performance, and information generated during reading checkpoints.

Some of this information is stored locally on the device, while supported profile and learning information can be synchronized through Firebase services as described in this Privacy Policy.

Aksharr does not use children's information for advertising.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of the app and to contact us if they have questions about information associated with a child's use of Aksharr.

Third-Party Services

Aksharr uses the following external services to provide specific functionality:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — account authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — synchronization of supported profile and learning information.
  • Google Sign-In — optional account authentication.
  • Apple Speech Recognition — speech-to-text processing during reading checkpoints.
  • Apple iCloud services — synchronization of supported app settings and, where configured and available, app data.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Aksharr does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to the service providers described in this Privacy Policy when necessary to provide authentication, synchronization, or speech-recognition functionality.

Profile photographs, scanned document images, OCR-extracted text, and PencilKit handwriting data were not observed being uploaded to the app's Firebase services.

Aksharr does not share children's information with advertising networks.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Aksharr generally remains on the device until it is deleted through an available in-app deletion function or the app is uninstalled.

Scanned documents can be deleted individually from within the Scan & Read section.

Aksharr also provides an account deletion function. The current app implementation deletes the local child data and attempts to delete the Firebase Authentication account.

Important:

The current project does not confirm that all associated Firestore documents are automatically deleted when an account is deleted. Cloud data deletion may therefore require additional server-side cleanup.

Because the exact retention of remote Firebase data depends on the application's Firebase configuration and deletion processes, we do not make a blanket claim that all remote data is immediately and permanently deleted when an account is removed.

iCloud and Cloud Synchronization

Aksharr uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage for certain app settings and progress-related information.

The project also uses Apple's NSPersistentCloudKitContainer architecture for Core Data synchronization.

Whether full Core Data CloudKit synchronization is enabled for a particular production build depends on the app's iCloud entitlements and the user's iCloud configuration.

Security

Aksharr uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections when communicating with Firebase and Google services.

Firebase authentication tokens are handled by the Firebase SDK using the iOS Keychain.

The current implementation stores the optional parental PIN in local UserDefaults and also synchronizes it with Firestore. UserDefaults is not intended as encrypted storage for sensitive information.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

Depending on the feature, you may be able to:

  • Delete scanned documents from within the app.
  • Remove your account using the account deletion option provided in the app.
  • Control device permissions such as camera, microphone, speech recognition, and photo access through iOS settings.
  • Choose whether to use optional Google Sign-In or another available authentication method.

If you have a question about information associated with your account or child profile, you can contact us using the address below.
